KNOWLEDGE BASE

Questions About Architectural Pool Design

Our editorial team answers the most important questions from discerning homeowners considering a bespoke aquatic commission.

Explore Our Services

A bespoke AQUORA pool commission typically spans 14–26 weeks from design approval to first fill, depending on the complexity of the architectural design, site conditions, permitting jurisdiction, and the scope of integrated features such as infinity edges, grottos, or spa systems. Our turnkey project management ensures each phase proceeds on schedule with zero client-side administrative burden.

Infinity edge pools require a precisely engineered catch basin, a dedicated high-capacity recirculation pump, and a vanishing-edge wall built to within millimeter tolerances to achieve the seamless overflow effect. The structural engineering involves cantilever calculations for cliff-edge installations, reinforced gunite formations, and a hydraulic balance system that prevents backflow during wind events — all of which demand specialist engineering expertise that goes well beyond standard pool construction.

Our most-selected stone finishes include Turkish travertine (its natural thermal properties keep the surface cool underfoot), French Vals limestone for understated sophistication, Italian quartzite for high-wear durability, and sandblasted granite for contemporary estates. All stones are sealed with our proprietary micro-crystalline treatment, which provides UV and chlorine resistance without altering the stone's natural character or tactile warmth.

Our lighting design process begins with a dedicated photometric study during the architectural phase. We layer three tiers of illumination: in-pool chromatherapy LED niche lights, perimeter uplighting on landscape features, and integrated hardscape step lighting. All systems are controlled through a smart home-integrated scene controller, allowing pre-programmed mood sequences — from a tranquil blue dawn palette to a dramatic sunset amber theatre — at the touch of a single button.

Every AQUORA commission carries a 25-year structural warranty on the gunite shell and a 10-year finish warranty on all applied stone and tile work. Equipment packages are warranted at 5 years with a dedicated white-glove service concierge available 24/7. Our AIA escrow-backed payment milestones and fixed-price contracts ensure complete financial transparency throughout the build, eliminating the cost overruns that plague conventional pool contractors.
