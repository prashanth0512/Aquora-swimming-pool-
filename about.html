OUR STORY

Born from a Passion for Architectural Water

Founded in 2001 by Marcus Aldren, AQUORA was born from a conviction that private pools must be sculpted as legacy architectural art rather than standard utility amenities.

In 2008, our signature Pacific bluff cantilever won the AIA Hydraulic Excellence Award, elevating the atelier onto the international stage.

Today, 80+ architects, hydrodynamic engineers, and artisan stoneworkers collaborate under one philosophy: every waterscape is a permanent extension of the estate.

AIA Certified Studio

Every design exceeds American Institute of Architects structural standards.

25-Year Structural Guarantee

Industry-leading warranty covering hydraulics, shell integrity, and all finishes.

18 International Awards

Recognised by Architectural Digest, AIA, and the Global Luxury Pool Council.

We exist to transform outdoor spaces into permanent, legacy-grade waterscapes — each one calibrated precisely to the geological, aesthetic, and cultural identity of its estate. We refuse to deliver less than extraordinary.

OUR JOURNEY

25 Years of Milestones

From a single Newport Beach studio to a globally recognized architectural atelier.

2001

Founded in Newport Beach

Marcus Aldren establishes AQUORA from a coastal studio with three structural engineers and one guiding principle: water is architecture.

Foundation
2005

First International Commission

Completed the Côte d'Azur Terrace project in Nice, France — a 40-metre perimeter-overflow mirror pool overlooking the Mediterranean.

Milestone
2008

First AIA Excellence Award

The Pacific Bluff Cantilever — a 64-foot infinity edge over a 280-foot sheer cliff — earns the first AIA Hydraulic Excellence Award and international press coverage.

Award
2012

AQUORA Digital Platform Launched

Pioneered the industry's first real-time client construction portal — giving patrons drone-fed milestone progress, live water chemistry, and direct architect access.

Innovation
2016

Architectural Digest Cover Feature

The Scottsdale Courtyard Pavilion — a zero-edge liquid mirror pool with Roman travertine colonnades — is the cover story of Architectural Digest's annual water issue.

Recognition
2020

100th Estate Commission Delivered

Marked the delivery of the 100th bespoke estate commission — a resort-scale lagoon system in Mustique — attended by 14 world-class architects.

Milestone
2024

Global Luxury Pool Council Award

Named the world's most awarded private pool atelier — 340+ completed estates across 9 countries and 18 architectural awards in 23 years of mastery.
