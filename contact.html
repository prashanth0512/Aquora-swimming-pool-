CLARITY & PROTOCOL

Client Inquiries & Atelier Protocol

Everything you need to know about initiating a bespoke architectural commission with AQUORA.

Our turnkey architectural projects typically begin at $150,000 for foundational inground and slot-drain installations, with vanishing-edge cantilevers and complex cliffside hydraulic engineering ranging upwards of $350,000 to $1,500,000+. Every masterplan proposal is backed by our fixed-price turnkey guarantee.

Over 70% of our commissions are collaborative partnerships with world-class residential architectural studios and landscape architects. We provide full BIM (Building Information Modeling) Revit and CAD integrations, hydraulic flow simulations, and structural engineering calculations to integrate seamlessly into your master architectural drawing package.

Yes. AQUORA maintains an internal regulatory expedition team with specialized expertise in complex jurisdictions—including California Coastal Commission approvals, hillside geological review boards, and historic preservation districts. We manage the entire submittal, engineering stamping, and inspection process turnkey.

Through our private Patron Ambassador Network, we can arrange confidential private walkthroughs of completed estate commissions in Newport Coast, Malibu, and Palm Beach, subject to homeowner schedule and prior non-disclosure clearance. Additionally, all three of our ateliers maintain working 1:1 scale finish mockups and VR simulation holodecks.

Every AQUORA commission is governed under standard AIA fixed-price contracts. Payments are strictly milestone-gated (e.g., Shell Pour, Hydraulic Rough-in, Stone Coping, Commissioning & Handover) and held in insured third-party construction escrow. No advance retainers are released until objective independent engineering sign-offs are verified.

All AQUORA pools are backed by our industry-leading 25-Year Structural Monolithic Shell Warranty and a 5-year comprehensive equipment and hydraulic piping warranty. Every estate patron receives dedicated 24/7 access to our service concierge, seasonal winterization and startup services, and automated remote water chemistry monitoring.
